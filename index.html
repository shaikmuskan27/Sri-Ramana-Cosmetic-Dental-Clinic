Got Questions? We Have Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Everything you need to know about our dental treatments, ceramic implants, smile design, clinic procedures, and visiting Dr. Sri Ramana in Nandyal.

Dr. Sri Ramana provides four primary specialized disciplines:

  • Cosmetic Dentistry: Smile makeovers, bespoke porcelain veneers, and composite bonding.
  • Ceramic & Dental Implants: Specialized metal-free zirconia implant placement and permanent fixed teeth solutions.
  • Laser Procedures: Advanced modern dental treatments for soft tissue sculpting, periodontal care, and whitening.
  • Routine Care: General check-ups, ultrasonic cleaning, and preventive dental maintenance.

In addition, we offer painless single-sitting root canals (RCT) and CAD/CAM monolithic zirconia crowns.

Ceramic and Zirconia implants are 100% metal-free, hypoallergenic, and bio-inert. Unlike traditional metal implants, ceramic implants are tooth-colored (natural ivory), eliminating the risk of dark gray margins showing through your gums. They attract significantly less bacterial plaque, promoting healthier long-term gum tissue.

No, the procedure is minimally invasive and virtually painless. We utilize high-precision micro-preparation techniques that preserve maximum healthy natural tooth enamel. Computer-guided local anesthesia ensures that you stay comfortable and relaxed during the entire appointment.

Laser dental procedures provide exceptional surgical accuracy with minimal to no bleeding, reduced swelling, and accelerated recovery times. Because the laser beam sterilizes tissue as it operates, infection risk is minimized. Laser dentistry is ideal for gum depigmentation, periodontal pocket disinfection, gummy smile corrections, and rapid in-office teeth whitening.

We advise visiting every 6 months for general dental examinations and ultrasonic scaling (teeth cleaning). Routine maintenance eliminates hardened plaque (tartar) that regular brushing cannot remove, prevents periodontal disease, and identifies micro-cavities before they turn into major dental problems.
